# IDAM JAGATH – The Invasion (original concept, as pitched 2026-11-05)

**An Indian-origin, browser-first retro FPS game.**

## Concept
A retro-inspired, fully 3D first-person shooter set during an alien invasion of Earth. It combines the atmosphere of classic Quake-style FPS games with modern visual polish, creative monsters, immersive environments and short, action-packed missions. Anyone can play instantly from a browser link: no downloads, no long installs, no required account, no grinding.

## Story
Aliens have invaded Earth. Large populations are held captive in occupied territories, guarded by dangerous alien creatures, and the aliens are slowly converting captured humans into monsters. Humanity survives in fortified shelters and sends fighters into occupied territory to eliminate alien threats, disable conversion facilities and rescue survivors. The player is one of those fighters.

## Gameplay
- Missions set in recognisable real-world locations (Taj Mahal, Times Square, Gateway of India)
- Unique monsters with abilities players must observe, understand and counter
- Rescue missions: defeat guards, destroy alien machinery, free captives
- Short sessions of about 5–10 minutes
- Solo PvE first, then optional co-op, eventually PvP

## Visual direction
A modern take on Quake-era 3D: atmospheric low-poly environments, detailed memorable monsters, dark sci-fi lighting and fog, distinctive weapons and effects, and real architecture transformed by alien occupation. Nostalgic, but with modern artistic quality.

## Platform
- Browser first. Desktop: WASD + mouse. Mobile: virtual joystick + touch aiming. Fullscreen; shareable via WhatsApp and Discord; no login needed to play.
- Native Android/iOS later if the browser version finds an audience, reusing a shared asset library.

## Technology
Babylon.js / TypeScript, Claude Code, Meshy or Tripo AI for 3D models, Blender for cleanup, AI image generation for concept art.

## Initial MVP
One compact alien-occupied level inspired by an Indian landmark; one primary weapon; two regular enemy types plus one special monster; a captive-rescue objective; one surprise encounter and one hidden secret; a complete ~5-minute mission; desktop browser first, then mobile touch.

## Long term: the Idam universe
- **Game 1: IDAM JAGATH – The Invasion.** Humanity fights to take back Earth.
- **Sequel: IDAM VISWAM.** The war expands to other planets and alien civilisations.

> Open a link. Enter a world. Fight aliens. Rescue humanity. Leave whenever you want.
> **The world is lost. The fight is not.**
