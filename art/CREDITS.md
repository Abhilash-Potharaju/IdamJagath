## Poly Haven (CC0, attribution not required)
Textures, HDRIs and models fetched with `tools/assets/polyhaven_fetch.py` into `art/library/polyhaven/`.
The full list (source URL, authors, files) is in `art/library/polyhaven/manifest.json`. We credit Poly Haven
voluntarily in the game credits.

## Licenses we accept
- ✅ CC0, CC BY (credit required)
